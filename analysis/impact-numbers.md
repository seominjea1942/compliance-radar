# Impact numbers (two sourced facts for the pitch deck)

No TAM framing, no projections. Two facts with sources.

## 1. Scale: how many stores look like Sam's

From the U.S. Census Bureau, Statistics of U.S. Businesses (SUSB), 2021 Annual
Data Tables by Establishment Industry, table "U.S. & states, NAICS, detailed
employment sizes" (us_state_naics_detailedsizes_2021.xlsx), NAICS 445110
"Supermarkets and Other Grocery (except Convenience) Stores", United States:

- Grocery firms with FEWER THAN 50 employees: **35,155 firms** operating
  **35,482 establishments** (sum of the <20, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35-39, and
  40-49 enterprise-size rows).
- That is **92% of all 38,170 U.S. grocery firms** (which operate 62,329
  establishments in total; the remaining 8% of firms, the chains, operate the
  other ~26,800 stores).
- Sam's segment specifically (under 20 employees): 30,265 firms / 30,379
  establishments.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, SUSB Annual Data Tables 2021,
https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2021/econ/susb/2021-susb-annual.html
(file: https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb/tables/2021/us_state_naics_detailedsizes_2021.xlsx).
Note: SUSB 2021 is the latest vintage with the detailed-size national table
downloadable without an API key; County Business Patterns 2022 has comparable
size classes but the API now requires registration.

## 2. Cost of not knowing: a real California enforcement case

**Save Mart Supermarkets, LLC (Save Mart, Lucky, FoodMaxx) paid $1.6 million**
($1,560,000 in civil penalties and costs plus $40,000 in cy pres restitution),
announced May 31, 2024, to resolve claims that its California stores sold
expired over-the-counter medications, **baby food, and infant formula** past
their use-by dates, with violations dating back to at least 2018. The case was
brought by the District Attorneys of El Dorado, Marin, San Joaquin, Solano,
Sonoma, and Yolo counties.

Source (official): Sonoma County District Attorney press release,
https://da.sonomacounty.ca.gov/save-mart-supermarkets-to-pay-16-million-dollars-to-resolve-claims-they-sold-expired-over-the-counter-medications-baby-food-and-infant-formula-beyond-use-by-dates
Corroboration: Grocery Dive, https://www.grocerydive.com/news/save-mart-expired-products-settlement-case/718016/

Honest caveat for the pitch: Save Mart is a 194-store regional chain, not an
18-employee independent; small-store enforcement rarely gets a press release.
The pitch line that stays true: "product-dating and recall enforcement in
California is real, multi-county, and reaches back years; a chain can absorb
$1.6M and a dedicated compliance staff, an independent cannot."
